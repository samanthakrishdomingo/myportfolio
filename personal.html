Samantha R. Domingo


My name is Samantha Krish R. Domingo, I was born on August 22, 2009 and grew up in province of Marilao Bulacan but now i live in Barangay Botocan, Quezon City, a lively and tight‑knit community in Metro Manila. I’m the eldest of four siblings, which has shaped much of who I am today.Bilang panganay ako ay may reponsibilidad na bantayan and aking mga kapatid at gabayan din sila.

Nung nasa bulacan pako is nakatira ako sa bahay ng isa kong lola, doon natuto ako ng mga magandang asal at kung paano tumayo sa aking sarili. Pero hindi ibig sabihin non ay masaya ako sa aking buhay doon dahil malayo ako sa aking mga kapatid at sa aking magulang. Habang ako ay naroon ay hindi ko kinakaya ang kanilang pagtrato sakin na ako ba ay kanilang hindi kapamilya, mahirap...dahil wala akong magawa para sa aking sarili kundi umiyak na lamang.

Being the oldest of four, I quickly learned to balance schoolwork with small responsibilities at home—helping my siblings prepare for school, reminding them to finish homework, or splitting our meals fairly. With four kids in one household, sharing and caring became second nature.Simula nung tumira ako kasama ang aking pamilya, ako ay naging masaya at naging malaya...nagagawa ko ang mga gusto kong gawin dahil hindi naman ganon ka istrikto si mami sakin pero kahit ganon ako ay naging responsibilidad parin sa paguwi sa tamang oras sa napagusapan namin.

School is a short walk away, and I enjoy reading stories, especially fairy tales and watching kdramas on weekends. Although I'm still young,I dream big. I imagine becoming a Doctor-who helps sick people and support my family. I want to share kindness, friendships, and remind people that even small acts lke helping someone close to your heart-make a difference.
